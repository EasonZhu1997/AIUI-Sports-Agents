- `brand/hero.svg` and `brand/logo-retro-wearable-mascot.png`;
- `architecture/aiui-sports-agents-agent-hub-blue-ink.png` and
  `architecture/aiui-sports-agents-agent-hub-blue-ink-en-v1.png`;
- `architecture/aiui-sports-agents-home-overview-handdrawn-v3.png` and

  `marketing/aiui-smartrun-21day-market-plan-en-v1.png`.

The Chinese and English blue-ink diagrams explain the relationship among Run, Bike, Rower, Paddle, the shared Hub, licensing scopes, and the separate evidence gates. The matching release-stage diagrams live under `../articles/images/open-source-guide/06-release-stages-blue-ink-v2.png` and `../articles/images/open-source-guide/06-release-stages-blue-ink-en-v1.png`. They are not screenshots, benchmark evidence, device certification, or a statement of platform approval. Product and protocol names inside them are descriptive compatibility references; third-party marks remain with their owners.

The marketing overview presents the current multi-sport product direction, Agent coaching, and the documented integration relationships in one visual.

The future-ecosystem poster is a concept illustration for planned device, outdoor-sport, and GPX route directions; it is not implementation or hardware-validation evidence. The 21-day running poster describes a proposed outreach and partnership plan rather than confirmed partnerships.

Each language README uses artwork whose embedded labels match that README: English assets end in `-en-v1`, while the corresponding Chinese originals remain on `README.zh-CN.md`.

Only files explicitly tracked in Git are part of this public asset set. Drafts and unrelated local artwork in the same workspace are not published by this statement.

The project owner confirmed that the orange Run, Bike, Rower, and Paddle artwork
under `project-icons/` is owner-created and authorized for direct use in this
Hub. Rower and Paddle deliberately use different equipment shapes.
Their use is governed by `TRADEMARKS.md`, and the detailed provenance and
non-evidence boundary is recorded in `project-icons/README.md`.
