# AIBike trademark and naming policy

The source license does not grant rights to use `AIBike`, its product identity, or
maintainer branding as the primary name or mark of a commercial product or service.

Accurate, non-confusing references to this project and compatibility statements are
allowed where applicable law permits. Forks and modified releases should use a
clearly different primary name, identify their maintainer, describe modifications,
and avoid implying certification, sponsorship, or official status.

`AIUI`, `Rokid`, `Bluetooth`, `Garmin`, `FTMS`, and other third-party names belong
to their respective owners. Compatibility references do not imply endorsement.

Brand permission and source-code commercial permission are separate approvals.
