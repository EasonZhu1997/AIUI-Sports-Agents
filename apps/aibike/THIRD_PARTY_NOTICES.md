The cadence click source is derived from BigSoundBank sound 0468, published under
CC0 1.0 / public-domain dedication. See `assets/audio/NOTICE.md` for provenance,
transform notes, and SHA-256 values. Attribution is retained for auditability.

## Development dependencies

Development packages are installed from `package-lock.json` and are not copied into
the AIX. Their license metadata must be reviewed for the exact resolved versions
before redistributing development tooling. In particular, the current lockfile does
not record a license field for the `@yodaos-pkg/*` packages; this snapshot does not
grant rights in those packages.

Bluetooth specifications, product names, AIUI, Rokid, and Garmin references are
used only to describe interoperability and remain subject to their owners' rights.
